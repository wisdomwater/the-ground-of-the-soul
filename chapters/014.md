## Day 14 - The Temple Made Ready

> *The soul becomes a temple of God when love is given freely, without expectation of reward.*

When our Lord Jesus entered the temple, he drove out those who bought and sold within it. I tell you that this temple signifies your own soul, which God has fashioned in his likeness and desires to possess entirely. Consider carefully whom Christ drove away, for I am speaking not merely of those who do evil, but even of virtuous people who perform good works while expecting something from God in return. They pray, fast, and serve faithfully, yet inwardly they bargain with God, hoping their devotion will purchase his favor.

Such people are merchants in the temple of the soul. They imagine that what they offer belongs to them, forgetting that their very existence and every power to do good have already been received from God. How then can you place God in your debt? All that you possess comes from the divine generosity, and God remains free in all his actions. True love does not calculate what it will receive, but gives itself because love is its very nature.

I would have you examine even your noblest intentions. Perhaps you seek peace through prayer, wisdom through contemplation, or holiness through discipline. These are good desires, yet they may become obstacles when you cling to the benefits more closely than to God himself. Christ does not enter the temple merely to improve your possessions, but to free you from the need to possess anything, even the rewards of spiritual devotion.

Therefore, let your good works arise from love alone, without demanding that God repay you. When the merchants have departed, the temple becomes clear and spacious, and Christ may speak his eternal Word within it. Then your soul will offer its gifts freely, receiving everything as grace and returning everything in love.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Clear the temple of my soul*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let me love without reward*
