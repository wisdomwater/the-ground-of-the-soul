## Day 21 - The Divine Being

> *To know God truly is to discover that God is greater than anything the mind can know.*

I tell you that no person can say precisely what God is. However carefully you choose your words, however deeply you search your understanding, the divine being remains beyond all that you can conceive. You may call God good, wise, powerful, or loving, and these names speak of truths worthy of contemplation. Yet God possesses these perfections in a manner so far beyond your understanding that no name can contain what he is.

Consider how you come to know the things around you. You recognize them by their qualities, understand them through their causes, and distinguish one thing from another by the forms they possess. But God is the source of every cause, the ground of every form, and the being upon which all created things depend. He cannot be understood as one object among others, however great or excellent. Even the highest angelic understanding cannot comprehend the fullness of his nature.

For this reason, I would teach you a way of knowing that begins with unknowing. When you say that God is good, remember that his goodness exceeds every goodness you have encountered. When you call him light, remember that he surpasses every light your understanding can imagine. By releasing the limits of these descriptions, you approach the mystery more faithfully than by clinging to the images they awaken.

Do not be troubled when your understanding reaches its end. There is a wisdom in recognizing what cannot be grasped, and a humility that opens the soul to what lies beyond its powers. Let your mind seek truth with all its strength, but allow it also to rest before the mystery it cannot contain. For the God who exceeds your knowledge is no less present to the soul that receives him.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Beyond all I can know*
>
> **Exhale:** *I rest in Your mystery*
