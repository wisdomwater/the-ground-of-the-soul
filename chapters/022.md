## Day 22 - Before Names and Forms

> *Every name reveals something of God, yet the fullness of God remains beyond every name.*

I tell you that God is not merely good, wise, or loving as creatures are good, wise, or loving. In created things, these qualities appear in varying measures, and one may possess what another lacks. But in God there is no such division. His goodness is his wisdom, his wisdom is his love, and all that we call his perfections are one in the simplicity of his divine being. What God possesses, God is.

Consider how your understanding gives names to the things it encounters. You recognize a tree by its form, a person by their qualities, and a work of art by the beauty it reveals. These distinctions help you navigate the world, yet they cannot contain the One from whom every form and quality proceeds. God is the source of all that can be known, and no created likeness can fully express the divine nature.

This does not mean that the names of God are without value. When you speak of divine goodness, you recognize something true, for every good gift flows from the Father of lights. When you contemplate divine wisdom, you glimpse the eternal Word through whom all things are understood. Yet I would have you remember that the light shining through creation is not exhausted by what it illuminates. The hidden source remains greater than everything that proceeds from it.

Therefore, do not abandon the names by which you have learned to love God, but allow them to lead you beyond themselves. Let goodness awaken gratitude, wisdom deepen understanding, and love draw your soul toward its source. As your understanding becomes quiet before the mystery, you may discover that the God whom no name can contain is also the One who gives life to every name you speak.
