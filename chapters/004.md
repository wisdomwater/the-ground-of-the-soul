## Day 4 - The Depth Beneath the Powers

> *The soul has many powers, but its deepest ground lies beyond all that its powers can accomplish.*

Consider the many ways your soul reaches out into the world. Through your senses you perceive what surrounds you, through memory you preserve what has passed, and through understanding you seek the meaning of what you encounter. Your will moves toward what you desire, while your love binds you to what you hold dear. These are noble powers, given to the soul so that it may know, choose, and act within creation. Yet I tell you that none of these powers, however excellent, is the deepest ground of your being.

The soul has, as it were, two faces. One turns outward toward the world, where it exercises wisdom and virtue through the circumstances of ordinary life. The other gazes inward toward God, receiving a light that does not arise from the senses or from the soul's own activity. These are not two separate souls, but different orientations of the one life within you. Even while your outward powers are occupied, something deeper remains capable of receiving the divine.

Do not despise your understanding or imagine that love and willing are obstacles to God. Each has its proper work, and through them the divine goodness may flow into the world. Yet the intellect must learn to seek beyond the qualities by which it ordinarily recognizes things, reaching toward God in the simplicity of divine being.

You are more than what you remember, understand, desire, or accomplish. Beneath these changing activities lies a depth that cannot be measured by their success or failure. Learn to gather your powers without becoming confined by them, allowing the whole soul to turn toward the One from whom its life continually flows.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Beyond my thoughts and desires*
>
> **Exhale:** *I rest in the ground of my soul*
