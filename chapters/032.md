## Day 32 - The Four Steps of Detachment

> *The soul grows free as it releases not only what it possesses, but even the inward claim that these things belong to it.*

I tell you that detachment has four steps, through which the soul is drawn away from the passing things of creation and toward the freedom of God. The first step begins when you loosen your hold upon what is perishable. You recognize that possessions, pleasures, honors, and earthly circumstances cannot provide the lasting peace for which you were made. Though these things remain around you, their power over your heart begins to weaken.

In the second step, the soul is deprived of what it once held dear. What you have learned to release inwardly may now be taken from you outwardly, whether through changing circumstances or the natural passing of life. This deprivation may bring sorrow, yet it also reveals how deeply your peace has depended upon things that cannot remain. The soul is invited to discover a freedom that does not depend upon their return.

The third step reaches deeper still. The soul becomes so free from its former attachments that even the memory of possessing them no longer governs its inward life. It does not continually rehearse what has been lost or measure the present against what has passed. What once seemed necessary has ceased to occupy the heart, leaving it undivided in its desire for God.

But the fourth step surpasses all the others, for it rests in God himself. Here detachment is no longer merely the absence of earthly attachments, but participation in the divine freedom. The soul has become so simple and receptive that its beauty delights the heavenly King. Therefore, do not seek detachment merely for the sake of losing things. Seek the freedom through which nothing prevents your soul from belonging wholly to God.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Free me from what passes*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let my soul rest in You*
