## Day 36 - The Empty Temple

> *The soul becomes a dwelling for God when it ceases to bargain for what love would freely give.*

When our Lord Jesus entered the temple, he drove out those who bought and sold, declaring that the house belonged to God. I tell you that this temple is your own soul, fashioned in the likeness of its Creator and made to receive his presence. God desires to dwell there without rival, for no created thing can satisfy the deepest purpose of the soul. Yet you may fill this holy place with your own expectations, leaving little room for the freedom of divine love.

Consider the merchants whom Christ drove away. They represent not only those who seek worldly gain, but also those who perform good works expecting God to reward them. You may pray for peace, practice virtue for assurance, or serve others in the hope of becoming worthy of divine favor. Such works are good in themselves, yet when you make them instruments of exchange, your heart becomes a marketplace. You begin to treat God's generosity as something to be earned rather than freely received.

I would have you understand that everything you offer God has already come from God. Your life, your strength, and even your capacity to love are gifts that precede your efforts. How then can you place the Giver in your debt? The soul becomes truly poor when it relinquishes every claim upon God, no longer demanding consolation, certainty, or reward in exchange for its devotion.

Therefore, let the merchants depart from your inward temple. Continue to pray, to serve, and to practice goodness, but do these things from love itself, without calculating what you shall receive. For when the soul is emptied of its bargains, it becomes free for God to dwell and work within it. And where love acts without seeking its own advantage, the temple becomes a place of divine freedom.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Empty the temple of my heart*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let me love without reward*
