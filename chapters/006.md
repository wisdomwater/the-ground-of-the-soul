## Day 6 - The Spark That Remains

> *Even in the deepest darkness, the soul bears a spark that inclines it toward the light.*

I tell you that within the soul there is a spark that continually turns toward God. It is not one of the ordinary powers by which you remember, reason, or choose, but a light that draws the soul toward its highest good. Though you may become distracted by the world and forget the purpose for which you were made, this spark does not cease its inward calling. It remains a witness to the divine goodness, urging the soul toward what is true.

Consider how a small flame may kindle another, awakening fire where only a faint ember remained. So too the divine light enters the soul, drawing its hidden spark upward toward the source from which all light proceeds. Yet this light is not something you possess or produce by your own strength. It comes from God, and through its working the soul is invited to rise beyond its ordinary understanding into a deeper participation in divine life.

This spark performs a twofold work. It troubles you when you turn away from goodness, awakening sorrow for what is disordered within you. Yet it also continually calls you toward the good, stirring a desire that no earthly satisfaction can entirely fulfill. Even if a soul were sunk into the deepest misery, I say that this inward inclination toward goodness would remain. Such is the persistence of the light by which God summons the soul.

Therefore, do not despair when you discover how far your desires have wandered. Attend instead to that quiet movement within you that still longs for truth, goodness, and love. The spark is not the fullness of divine union, but it calls you toward the feast God has already prepared. Follow its light, and allow the goodness toward which it draws you to become the life you live.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Your light remains within me*
>
> **Exhale:** *Draw my soul toward the good*
