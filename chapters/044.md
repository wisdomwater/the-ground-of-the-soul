## Day 44 - The Renewal of the Mind

> *The soul is renewed not by becoming something other than itself, but by allowing every power within it to be transformed by divine love.*

Saint Paul calls you to be renewed in the spirit of your mind. Consider carefully what this renewal means, for God himself has no need of becoming new. In eternity there is no aging or passing away, no movement from what is incomplete toward what is perfect. Yet your soul lives amid change, and its powers must continually be brought into harmony with the divine life. What is eternal in God becomes the source of renewal within you.

I tell you that the soul possesses six powers, three lower and three higher, and each must wear a golden ring of divine love. Upon reason place the ring of light, that your judgments may be illuminated by truth. Upon anger place the ring of peace, that your spirit may remain steadfast in God. Upon desire place the ring of sufficiency, that created things may no longer command your heart, while your longing for God remains without limit.

The three higher powers must likewise be adorned. Let memory wear the ring of remembrance, preserving what belongs to eternity. Let understanding wear the ring of perception, seeking God beyond every image and likeness. And let the will wear the ring of love, not merely loving God for his goodness, but reaching beyond every quality toward the divine mystery itself.

Therefore, do not imagine renewal as the destruction of your human nature. Every power of the soul has its place, and each may become an instrument of divine life. Let your understanding be enlightened, your desires become free, and your will be united with love. For when the whole soul is gathered into God, its changing life begins to reflect the stillness of eternity.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Renew every power within me*
>
> **Exhale:** *Unite my soul in Your love*
