## Day 8 - The Place of True Rest

> *Every creature seeks rest, but the soul finds its deepest peace where God himself comes to rest.*

I tell you that all creatures seek rest, whether they know it or not. Every movement of the body, every longing of the heart, and every labor of the mind is directed toward some form of peace. You close your eyes to escape what troubles them, and open them to seek what brings delight. Even your restless striving is a search for something in which you may finally be satisfied. Yet the rest you seek cannot be fully found among created things, for everything in this world is subject to change.

Consider a pool of water stirred by the wind. While its surface is troubled, it cannot clearly reflect what stands above it. But when the water becomes still, the image appears without effort, as though it had been waiting there all along. So it is with the soul. When your inward powers are scattered among countless desires and concerns, the divine presence remains hidden beneath their movement. As the soul grows quiet and clear, it becomes capable of receiving what God longs to reveal.

And I say something more wonderful still: God also seeks rest within you. As the soul rests in God, so God rests in the soul. This is the mystery of their union, for the divine nature delights to dwell where the heart has become peaceful and free. God asks for a quiet dwelling, not because he lacks anything, but because his deepest work unfolds where the soul is no longer crowded by its own restless activity.

Therefore, withdraw for a little while from the tumult of outward things and the rushing of inward thoughts. Allow your desires to settle and your heart to become peaceful. For the rest you seek is also the place where God desires to dwell.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *My soul finds rest in You*
>
> **Exhale:** *May You find rest in me*
