## Day 12 - The Birth That Never Ceases

> *What is born in eternity does not pass away with time, for the life of God knows neither beginning nor end.*

I tell you that the Father gives birth to his Son in an eternal now. Do not imagine this birth as something that happened long ago, nor as an event that must be repeated whenever the soul turns toward God. In eternity there is neither before nor after, for God possesses the fullness of life without succession or change. The divine Word proceeds from the Father continually, and this eternal mystery may become present within the depths of your soul.

You are accustomed to understanding everything through time. A thought arises and passes away, a feeling awakens and fades, and even your most profound spiritual experiences eventually yield to the ordinary concerns of life. You may therefore suppose that the divine birth also comes and goes, appearing when you feel close to God and disappearing when your attention wanders. Yet I ask you to distinguish between the changing experience of divine life and the deeper union in which that life is received.

When the soul surrenders itself to God, it becomes receptive to a work that belongs to God alone. This birth may be fleeting in your thoughts and perceptions, for these are subject to change. But in the deepest ground of the soul, where God accomplishes his work, the birth possesses a more enduring character. Its life is not measured by the passing moments in which you become conscious of it.

Therefore, do not judge the presence of God by the intensity of your experiences. Let your soul return to its ground, where the eternal Word is received beyond the restless movement of time. What God brings forth is deeper than what you can feel or remember, and the life toward which you are drawn belongs to the eternity of God himself.
