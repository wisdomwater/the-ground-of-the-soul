## Day 13 - The Word at the Beginning

> *God speaks one eternal Word, and in that Word all things find their beginning and their life.*

In the beginning was the Word, as Saint John tells us. Yet I would have you understand that this beginning is not a moment long past, before the heavens and earth were made. God has spoken only one Word, his only-begotten Son, and in this Word he speaks all creatures without beginning or end. The Word proceeds from the Father while remaining eternally within him, for the divine life cannot be divided or diminished by what it brings forth.

You may encounter this Word in many ways. You receive him in the holy sacrament, hear him proclaimed through those who teach, and recognize him in the lives of those who have followed Christ. These are worthy ways of knowing, and you should not despise them. Yet I tell you of a fourth way, more inward and immediate, in which God speaks his eternal Word within the virgin soul, without sound or any outward instrument.

When this Word is spoken within you, no human language can adequately express what is received. The learned may offer explanations, and their wisdom may lead you toward understanding, but the soul taught by the Holy Spirit discovers what no teacher can fully communicate. It receives from God what belongs to God, entering a knowledge that exceeds the grasp of ordinary thought.

Therefore, make yourself free and receptive to this divine teaching. Allow God to work when and how he wills, without demanding that his presence conform to your expectations. The Word through whom all things were made is not far from you, waiting to be summoned by your effort. He is eternally spoken by the Father, and the soul that becomes open to him may receive that same Word in its deepest ground.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Speak Your eternal Word*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let my soul receive You*
