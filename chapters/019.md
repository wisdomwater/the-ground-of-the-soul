## Day 19 - The Word the Soul Speaks

> *When the soul receives the Word of God, it discovers a word of its own that becomes understanding and love.*

I tell you that the soul possesses a wonderful power to speak a word within itself. When you remember something you have known, your understanding brings forth an image of it, and what was hidden in memory becomes present to the mind. So it is also with the knowledge of God. For God is present in all things, and when the soul brings forth the divine image held within its memory, it speaks a word that bears a likeness to the eternal Word of the Father.

Consider how the powers of your soul work together in this mystery. Memory receives and preserves, understanding brings forth what memory contains, and the will moves toward what understanding has revealed. When the soul knows God in this way, the word born in the intellect becomes love within the will. Here you may glimpse a likeness of the Holy Trinity, for memory, understanding, and love reflect, in their created manner, the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Yet I would have you distinguish carefully between the Word that God eternally speaks and the word that arises within your soul. The Father brings forth his Son as a distinct divine Person, sharing fully in the one divine nature. Your soul does not bring forth another divine Person, but receives and reflects the eternal Word according to its own created capacity. The likeness is profound, yet the mystery of God remains greater than the image through which you participate in it.

Therefore, attend to what is taking shape within your understanding, for what you contemplate may become the love by which you live. Allow your memory to be filled with divine goodness, your intellect to be enlightened by truth, and your will to be moved toward love. For when God speaks his Word within you, your soul is invited to answer with its whole life.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Let Your Word awaken in me*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let my answer become love*
