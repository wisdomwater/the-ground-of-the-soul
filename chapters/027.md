## Day 27 - Divine Understanding

> *God knows himself in perfect simplicity, and the soul draws near to him by becoming simple in its own understanding.*

I tell you that God is a light shining within himself in eternal stillness. This light needs no other light by which to see, for God knows himself through his own divine nature. The Father beholds himself perfectly, and this eternal understanding is his Son, the Word in whom all things are known. Nothing is hidden from this understanding, for all creatures have their beginning in the one light from which they proceed.

Consider how differently you come to know the world. You examine one thing after another, gathering knowledge through your senses and forming judgments through your understanding. Your mind moves among many objects, seeking the truth that unites them. Yet in God there is no such movement from ignorance to knowledge. His understanding is his very being, and all things are known within the simplicity of his eternal life.

If you would enter more deeply into divine understanding, you must learn to become simple. By simplicity I do not mean ignorance or the abandonment of reason, but freedom from the multitude of attachments that divide your attention. When the soul releases its possessiveness, even toward its own understanding, it becomes receptive to a wisdom greater than anything it could produce. Then God works within the soul, and it begins to understand through the light he communicates.

And this divine understanding bears fruit in love. For God pours his own love into the soul through the Holy Spirit, enabling it to love with a love that has its source in God himself. Thus knowledge and love find their unity in the divine life. The more deeply you surrender to this simplicity, the more freely that life may unfold within you.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Gather me into Your simplicity*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let me know and love through You*
