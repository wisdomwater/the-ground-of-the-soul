## Day 7 - The Man in the Soul

> *The soul comes alive when its understanding rises beyond the gifts of God to rest in God himself.*

Consider the woman who came to the prophet seeking help, for her husband had died and her creditors threatened to take her children. She possessed almost nothing, only a little oil, yet the prophet instructed her to gather empty vessels and pour what remained into them. I see in this story a mystery of the soul, for the husband represents the intellect, that noble power through which the soul may turn toward God and receive the light of divine understanding.

I call this power the man in the soul, not because it belongs to one sex rather than another, but because it represents the soul's capacity to rise beyond created things into the knowledge of God. Love and will seek God as good and desirable, but the intellect presses deeper, seeking God beyond every quality by which he may be known. It longs for the divine essence itself, where goodness, wisdom, and power have their source.

The soul has two faces. One turns toward the world, exercising wisdom and virtue in the circumstances of daily life. The other gazes toward God, where divine light continually shines. When the intellect becomes absorbed in outward things alone, the soul forgets its deepest orientation. Yet when it turns toward God, something within it awakens, and the eternal birth takes place beyond the boundaries of time.

Therefore, gather the empty vessels of your soul, your senses, understanding, will, and all your inward powers. Let them become receptive to the wisdom that flows from God. For what you receive in the depths of your being is meant to fill the whole of your life, so that divine goodness may flow through you into everything you encounter.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Awaken Your light within me*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let Your wisdom flow through me*
