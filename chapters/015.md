## Day 15 - Children of God

> *To be called a child of God is not merely to receive a name, but to share in the life of the One who gives us birth.*

Saint John declares that the Father has bestowed such love upon us that we should be called children of God, and truly be so. Mark well these words, for he does not say that we merely bear the name of children. I tell you that no one can be wise without possessing wisdom, nor can anyone be a child of God without participating in the nature of God's Son. The gift is greater than a title or a promise of future happiness. It is an invitation into the very life of God.

You may ask how a creature can share in what belongs to the eternal Son. Consider the air when sunlight fills it. The air does not produce its own brightness, yet it becomes luminous through the light that shines within it. So too your soul knows God because God first makes himself known within you. Your knowing arises from his knowing, and your life is awakened through the life he communicates.

Yet this mystery remains hidden beneath the many images and distinctions by which you understand yourself. You regard yourself as separate, possessing your own goodness and standing apart from those around you. But in divine life, what belongs to one is not withheld from another. I tell you that the blessed share in one another's joy and grace, for all are gathered into the unity of God.

Therefore, release the narrow understanding by which you measure your worth and your separation from others. Let the soul become free from every image that confines its understanding of divine life. For you were not created merely to observe God from a distance, but to be transformed through participation in the life of his Son.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *I am called Your child*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let Your life be born in me*
