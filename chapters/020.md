## Day 20 - The Life of a New Beginning

> *The birth of God within the soul is not the end of its becoming, but the beginning of a life continually transformed by love.*

When Elizabeth's time was fulfilled, she brought forth a son, and those who heard of his birth wondered what manner of child he would become. I tell you that a greater wonder awaits within your own soul, for God desires to bring forth his eternal Son in you. This is the purpose toward which divine grace continually moves. God is not content merely to visit the soul with passing moments of consolation, but desires to communicate his own life until the whole person is transformed.

Consider a piece of wood placed within a fire. At first the wood resists the flame, for its nature differs from that of the fire. Gradually it grows warm, then hot, until it begins to burn. As the fire penetrates more deeply, the wood loses its former qualities and becomes increasingly like the flame itself. So it is with the soul that receives divine love. What begins as a gentle awakening may gradually transform the whole of your being.

Do not suppose that this work must be completed in a single moment. A soul may spend many years growing in love, and still find that greater love remains possible. The Holy Spirit breathes upon the inward flame, awakening new capacities for goodness, wisdom, and joy. Grace works according to the soul's readiness, drawing it beyond its former limits without exhausting the riches of divine life.

Therefore, rejoice in every beginning, however small it may appear. Do not measure the divine work solely by what has already changed within you. Give yourself freely to the love that kindles your soul, trusting that the fire which awakens you also possesses the power to transform you.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Kindle Your love within me*
>
> **Exhale:** *Transform me in Your fire*
