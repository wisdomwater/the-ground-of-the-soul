## Day 11 - What Good Is a Birth Not Born in Me?

> *The eternal birth of God is of little benefit to the soul unless that same birth takes place within it.*

You celebrate the birth of Christ and rejoice that the eternal Son of God entered human history. This is a great and holy mystery, worthy of all your praise. Yet I ask you to consider something further. What good is it to you that the Son was born long ago in Bethlehem if he is not also born within your own soul? The birth that took place in time must become a living reality within you, or its deepest meaning remains undiscovered.

I tell you that the Father is eternally giving birth to his Son. This divine act has no beginning and no end, for it belongs to the very life of God. And what is most wonderful is that the same Father who gives birth to his Son in eternity desires to bring forth that Son within the ground of your soul. This is not another birth separate from the eternal mystery, but your participation in the one divine life.

You may know much about Christ, speak eloquently of his teachings, and faithfully observe the celebrations of the Church. These things are good, yet they cannot take the place of the inward transformation for which you were created. The divine birth is not something you accomplish through knowledge or devotion alone. It is God's own work, received in the soul that has become quiet, free, and wholly receptive to his presence.

Therefore, do not be content merely to remember what God has done in ages past. Turn toward the living mystery that God desires to accomplish within you now. For the eternal Word is not confined to a distant time or place. The Father who gave his Son to the world desires also to give him birth in the hidden depths of your own being.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *You are eternally giving life*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let Your Son be born in me*
