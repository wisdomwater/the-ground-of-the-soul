## Day 24 - Seeing and Knowing

> *The soul does not see God by possessing knowledge, but by becoming free to receive the light in which God is known.*

I tell you that all people desire happiness, yet few understand where their happiness is found. You may seek it in the possession of good things, in the approval of others, or even in the satisfaction of your own understanding. But the blessedness of the soul lies in beholding God, not as one thing among many, but as the source in whom all things possess their being. For this reason I call the intellect a noble power, because it reaches beyond what the will desires and seeks to understand the truth itself.

Consider how your eyes receive the light of the world. What you see must pass through the air and light before it becomes known to your sight. In a similar way, the soul requires divine grace to become capable of beholding God. Yet this grace does not merely provide another image for your understanding. It prepares the soul to see beyond the images and distinctions that ordinarily stand between itself and the divine.

You may ask what prevents you from seeing God more clearly. Recall the man who climbed a tree to see Jesus above the surrounding crowd. I tell you that the crowd signifies the multitude of created things that occupy your attention and confine your desire. To see Christ, you must rise beyond their distractions, yet you must also hear his command to come down. For God desires to dwell within the humble soul, not merely to be observed from a distance.

Therefore, seek understanding with an ardent heart, but join your desire to humility. Let your knowledge lead you toward God without imagining that it can contain him. The highest vision comes when the soul is made ready by grace to receive what its own powers could never possess.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Open my eyes to Your light*

> **Exhale:** *Let me see with humble love*
