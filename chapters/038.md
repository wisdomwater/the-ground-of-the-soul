## Day 38 - Seeking Without Grasping

> *The soul draws near to God through ardent desire, yet discovers that the One it seeks can never be contained by its understanding.*

Consider the man Zacchaeus, who desired to see Jesus but could not because of the crowd. Being small of stature, he ran ahead and climbed a tree, hoping to look upon the Lord as he passed. I tell you that this man reveals something of the soul's journey toward God. The crowd represents the many created things that occupy your attention, while his eagerness reveals the desire by which the soul rises beyond them.

If you would see God, you must learn to pass beyond the things that hold you captive. Yet I do not ask you to abandon creation as though it were evil, for every creature receives its being from God. Rather, let your desire become so deeply rooted in divine goodness that no lesser satisfaction can claim your whole heart. The more the soul receives of God, the more its capacity grows, like a vessel that becomes larger as it is filled.

But consider what happened when Zacchaeus climbed above the crowd. Jesus called him to come down, declaring that he would dwell in his house. Here I would have you learn that ardent desire must be joined to humility. You cannot seize God by climbing higher in your own estimation, nor can the sharpest intellect contain the divine mystery. I tell you that God exceeds even the highest thing you can conceive.

Therefore, seek God with all the strength of your longing, while remaining humble before what you cannot grasp. Let your desire carry you beyond distractions, and let humility make your soul a welcoming dwelling. For the One whom your understanding cannot contain desires to make his home within you.
