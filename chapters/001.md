## Day 1 - The Word in the Silence

> *In the deepest silence of the soul, God speaks a Word that cannot be heard by the ears.*

There is a place within you that lies beyond the restless movement of thought, memory, and desire. It is not a physical place, nor something you can discover by searching more intensely. I call it the ground of the soul, the innermost depth of your being where no image or created thing can enter. Here, beneath the many activities of the mind, the soul rests in a stillness that is receptive to God alone.

I tell you that a mysterious birth takes place in this hidden ground. Just as the Father eternally brings forth the Son, so the divine Word may be born within your soul. Do not imagine this as a passing inspiration or a sudden moment of religious understanding. It is a participation in the divine life itself, a mystery that exceeds what your mind can grasp or your imagination can picture.

You may spend much of your life approaching God through thoughts and images. You contemplate divine wisdom, imagine divine beauty, and search for words to express your devotion. These things have their place, but they cannot carry you into the deepest mystery. For the soul to receive what God longs to give, it must become quiet, releasing even its cherished ideas of what God should be. The eternal Word is not born through your striving, but through the soul's openness to divine action.

Therefore, do not struggle to manufacture this birth or force the mystery into being. Turn inward and allow your scattered attention to settle. Become still, become receptive, and let the ground of your soul be free from all that would occupy it. For in the silence where your own words cease, the eternal Word may be heard.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *In the silence of my soul*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let Your Word be born in me*
