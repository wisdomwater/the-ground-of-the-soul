## Day 1 - The Word in the Silence

> *In the deepest silence of the soul, God speaks a Word that cannot be heard by the ears.*

There is a place within you that lies beyond the restless movement of thought, memory, and desire. It is not a physical place, nor something you can discover by searching more intensely. Meister Eckhart calls it the ground of the soul, the innermost depth of your being where no image or created thing can enter. Here, beneath the many activities of the mind, the soul rests in a stillness that is receptive to God alone.

Eckhart speaks of a mysterious birth that takes place in this hidden ground. Just as the Father eternally brings forth the Son, so the divine Word may be born within the soul. This is more than an experience of inspiration or a sudden moment of religious understanding. It is a participation in the divine life itself, a mystery that exceeds what the mind can grasp or the imagination can picture.

Yet we often approach God by filling our minds with thoughts about God. We reflect upon divine wisdom, imagine divine beauty, or search for the right words to express our devotion. These have their place, but Eckhart invites us to recognize their limits. The deepest encounter with God does not arise from our ability to construct an image of the divine. It requires a quieting of the soul's outward activity, a willingness to become still and receptive to what only God can accomplish.

You need not manufacture the Word or force the mystery into being. Your part is to turn inward, gathering your scattered attention into the quiet depths of the soul. There, beyond the familiar noise of your own making, you may become receptive to the eternal Word that God alone can speak.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *In the silence of my soul*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let Your Word be born in me*
