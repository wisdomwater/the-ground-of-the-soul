## Day 43 - An Image Worth Becoming

> *The image of God is not something the soul must create, but a likeness already given that longs to return to its source.*

I tell you that when God created the soul, he fashioned her according to his own image. Consider what this means, for an image possesses nothing of itself apart from the one whose likeness it bears. Its nature is to reveal another, and its perfection lies in faithfully reflecting its source. So it is with your soul, which has received a likeness of God that no created thing can bestow and no outward power can fully comprehend.

Consider a mirror held before your face. The image that appears within it does not arise from the mirror's own nature, but from the presence of the one reflected there. In a similar way, the divine image within your soul depends entirely upon God. It proceeds from him, bears his likeness, and continually inclines toward the One from whom it came. Yet this image is not found in the changing activities of memory, understanding, or will. It lies hidden in a deeper power, where the soul receives the light of God without anything standing between them.

You may search for this likeness among your accomplishments, your virtues, or the qualities by which you distinguish yourself from others. Yet none of these can contain the image of which I speak. Your outward powers change with the passing of time, but the divine likeness calls you toward what is eternal. The more freely you release your attachment to created things, the more clearly your soul may turn toward its source.

Therefore, do not labor to manufacture the image of God within yourself. Rather, become free from what obscures its light, allowing your soul to reflect the One whose likeness it already bears. For the image finds its fulfillment when nothing stands between the soul and God.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Your image lives within me*
>
> **Exhale:** *Draw me closer to Your likeness*
