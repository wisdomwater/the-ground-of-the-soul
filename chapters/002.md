## Day 2 - Where No Image Enters

> *The soul becomes free not by forgetting the world, but by ceasing to possess what it knows.*

Consider how many images you carry within yourself. You remember the faces of those you love, the places you have visited, the joys you have experienced, and the sorrows you have endured. Your mind gives form to everything it encounters, and through these forms you come to understand the world. Yet I tell you that in the deepest ground of the soul there is a place where no image can enter, for this ground belongs to God alone.

You may wonder how anyone who has lived and learned can become free of images. Must you forget everything you have known, abandon your understanding, or empty your memory of those you love? Certainly not. I speak of a freedom deeper than forgetfulness. Even if you possessed knowledge of every created thing, you could remain inwardly free, provided you claimed nothing as your own and allowed nothing to bind your will.

For it is not the presence of images that prevents the soul from receiving God, but the attachment that makes them its possession. When you cling to your ideas, your achievements, or even your understanding of the divine, you fill the inward chamber with things that cannot contain its true treasure. The soul must become like a clear and open dwelling, ready to receive what no created form can give.

Do not mistake this freedom for indifference toward the world. A soul that receives God must also become fruitful, allowing divine life to take expression in gratitude, love, and action. The inward emptiness of which I speak is therefore not barren. It is the spaciousness through which God may enter, dwell, and bring forth life.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Make my soul an open dwelling*
>
> **Exhale:** *Free me from all I cling to*
