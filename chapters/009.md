## Day 9 - The One Power in the Soul

> *Beyond all that the mind can conceive and the heart can desire, there is a depth where God alone may dwell.*

I tell you that there is a power within the soul more spacious than the heavens, more expansive than anything the mind can measure. Though you may search for its boundaries, you will not find them, for this power reaches beyond the limits of time and place. It is so pure and so noble that no created thing can dwell within it. God alone abides there, not as an image or a quality you might recognize, but in the simplicity of his divine being.

Consider how you seek God through the things you understand and desire. You imagine goodness, beauty, wisdom, and love, and you stretch your thoughts toward their highest perfection. Yet whatever your mind can conceive or your heart can desire is still not God as he is in himself. Where understanding reaches its end and desire can go no further, there remains a darkness in which the divine light shines beyond all knowing.

You may wonder how such a hidden power can be awakened. Recall the young man whom Christ raised from death, saying, “Young man, I say unto thee, arise.” I tell you that this command is spoken also within your soul. When you turn away from the restless pursuit of outward things and become inwardly free, the eternal Word calls you into a life that does not depend upon the changing circumstances of the world.

Do not imagine that you must accomplish this awakening by your own strength. The One who commands the soul to rise is also the One who gives it life. Your task is to become receptive, to release whatever confines your understanding, and to cease resisting the divine work. For where your own powers come to rest, God may reveal the life that has no beginning and no end.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Awaken the depths of my soul*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let Your life arise in me*
