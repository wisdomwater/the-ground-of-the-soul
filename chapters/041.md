## Day 41 - The Three Powers of the Soul

> *The soul is made to receive truth, behold its source, and bring forth love.*

I tell you that the soul possesses three noble powers through which it may participate in the life of God. The first receives, the second understands, and the third loves. These powers are not separate lives within you, but belong to the unity of your soul. Each has its proper work, and together they prepare the soul to receive the divine life for which it was created.

Consider how the soul receives what comes before it. Through memory and inward attention, it gathers what has been encountered and preserves it within. Understanding then turns toward what has been received, seeking the truth beneath its outward appearance. Finally, the will moves toward what understanding has revealed, embracing through love what it recognizes as good. When these powers are directed toward created things alone, the soul remains occupied with what is passing. But when they turn toward God, they begin to participate in something eternal.

Yet I tell you that even these noble powers cannot bring the soul to its highest rest through their own activity. Receiving, understanding, and loving involve movement, as the soul reaches toward what it seeks. Beyond this movement lies the stillness of divine unity, where God accomplishes his own work within the soul. There the Father eternally brings forth his Son, and the Holy Spirit communicates the love in which the soul finds its fulfillment.

Therefore, do not despise the powers through which you think, remember, choose, and love. Allow them to become ordered toward God, so that their activity may prepare you for deeper stillness. For the soul reaches its perfection when all its powers are gathered into the divine life, and their restless movement comes to rest in God.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Gather every power of my soul*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let them find their rest in You*
