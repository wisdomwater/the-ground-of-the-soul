## Day 10 - The Nobility Within

> *The soul discovers its true nobility when it recognizes the divine likeness from which it was formed.*

Beloved, learn to know yourself, for this knowledge will profit you more than any skill you may acquire in the world. You have been fashioned with a nobility greater than you imagine, bearing within your soul the likeness of the God who created you. Yet this dignity is easily forgotten when you measure yourself by outward appearances, possessions, or accomplishments. To know yourself truly, you must look beyond these passing things and discover the nature of the life that has been entrusted to you.

Consider the marvelous powers of your soul. Through memory you preserve what you have encountered, through understanding you discern what is true, and through the will you turn toward what you love. These powers are distinct in their work, yet they belong to one undivided soul. In this unity of diverse powers, I perceive a likeness to the Holy Trinity, whose three Persons share one divine nature. The soul is not God by its own nature, but it has been created with the capacity to receive divine life through grace.

And what a wonder this is! God has formed you from nothing, yet has endowed you with the capacity to know and love the One from whom all being proceeds. When divine understanding enters the soul, it awakens love, and that love overflows through every power of your being. What begins in the hidden depths gradually transforms the outward life, so that your thoughts, desires, and actions may become expressions of divine goodness.

Therefore, do not regard yourself as something small or insignificant. Neither imagine that your nobility makes you independent of the One who gives you life. The soul finds its highest dignity in receiving what it cannot create for itself, becoming ever more fully the likeness of the God from whom it came.
