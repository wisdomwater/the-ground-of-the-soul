## Day 37 - The Self That Must Be Released

> *To lose yourself in God is not to destroy what you are, but to become free from all that keeps you from your true nature.*

Our Lord declares that whoever loves his life in this world shall lose it, while whoever relinquishes that life shall preserve it unto eternity. These words may trouble you, for how can you be asked to hate the soul that God himself has created? I tell you that Christ does not command you to despise the life you have received, but to release the narrow manner in which you have come to possess it. The soul must become free from its attachment to itself if it would discover the fullness of its life in God.

Consider how firmly you cling to the person you believe yourself to be. You cherish your opinions, defend your desires, and seek to preserve the image through which others recognize you. Even your virtues may become possessions, for you take satisfaction in being wise, generous, or devout. Yet so long as the soul belongs chiefly to itself, it has not surrendered wholly to God. What you must relinquish is the claim that your life exists for your own sake alone.

When Christ calls you to deny yourself and take up your cross, he invites you into this freedom. The cross is not merely the suffering that comes upon you, but the willing surrender of whatever prevents divine love from governing your life. As the soul releases its possessiveness, it discovers that its truest nature was never contained within the small boundaries of self-concern.

Therefore, do not fear the loss of what cannot endure. Let your false claims fall away, and entrust your soul to the One from whom it receives its being. For in surrendering the life you have tried to possess, you become free to receive the life that God eternally gives.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *I release my claim upon myself*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let my life belong to You*
