## Day 17 - When the Word Is Conceived

> *The eternal Word is conceived in the soul when it ceases to claim its own work and becomes wholly receptive to God.*

I tell you that the eternal Word desires to be born within your soul, just as Christ was born of the Virgin Mary. Yet you may wonder how to prepare for such a birth, and what you must accomplish to make yourself worthy of it. I say that the soul must first become pure in its intentions, peaceful in its inward life, and free from the clamor of created things. For while the soul is occupied with speaking its own word, even the noblest word of its understanding, it cannot fully receive the Word that the Father longs to speak.

Consider how readily your mind fills itself with activity. You examine your thoughts, measure your progress, and seek to understand the mysteries of God. Even your desire for holiness may become another occupation of the self, as though divine life were something you could bring forth through sufficient effort. Yet the birth of the eternal Word requires a different disposition. The soul must relinquish its claim to govern the work, allowing God to accomplish what no created power can achieve.

This surrender does not mean that you should cease desiring God. Desire is the root of virtue, and the soul must lift its understanding toward the highest truth. But having turned toward God, it must become willing to receive rather than possess. As Mary offered herself to the divine mystery, so must you allow your own will to become spacious enough for God's will to unfold.

Therefore, cultivate a quiet and receptive heart. Let your understanding seek the truth without demanding mastery over it, and let your desire for God deepen into humble openness. For when the soul releases its own word, the Father may speak his eternal Word within it.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Quiet the words within me*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let Your Word be conceived*
