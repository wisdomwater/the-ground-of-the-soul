## Day 31 - What Detachment Means

> *True detachment is not having nothing, but being held captive by nothing.*

I have searched the writings of philosophers and sages, the teachings of the prophets, and the words of Christ, seeking the highest virtue by which the soul may be united with God. And I tell you that I have found none greater than detachment. For love draws the soul toward God, and humility lowers it before God, but detachment prepares a dwelling in which God alone may enter. It frees the soul from everything that would occupy the place belonging to the divine.

Do not imagine that detachment requires you to abandon your home, your work, or those whom you love. A person may possess nothing and still be consumed by desire, while another may live among many responsibilities and remain inwardly free. The freedom of which I speak is not measured by what surrounds you, but by what possesses your heart. When you cling to something as necessary for your peace, you surrender a portion of your inward freedom to it.

Consider how readily the soul becomes entangled in what it desires and fears. You seek approval, resist disappointment, and struggle to preserve the circumstances that bring you comfort. Even your longing for spiritual perfection may become another possession to defend. Detachment asks that you release these claims, allowing the soul to remain steadfast amid the changing conditions of life.

I tell you that God delights to dwell in the detached heart, for such a heart has become simple and open to his presence. This freedom does not diminish love, but purifies it, allowing you to receive and give without seeking to possess. Let the world remain in your care, but do not allow it to rule your soul. For where nothing created holds you captive, you become free to belong wholly to God.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Free my heart from clinging*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let me rest in You alone*
