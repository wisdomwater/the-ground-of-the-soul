## Day 40 - Freedom from Spiritual Possession

> *The soul is most free when it no longer seeks to possess even its own freedom.*

I tell you that detachment is the highest preparation of the soul for union with God. Yet there is a subtle danger in this teaching, for you may begin to regard detachment itself as something to acquire. You may examine your heart, measure how little you desire, and take satisfaction in the freedom you believe you have attained. But the moment you claim this freedom as your possession, you have become attached to the very thing that was meant to release you.

Consider the Virgin Mary, who possessed perfect humility and detachment. When she praised God for regarding her lowliness, she did not boast of her freedom from created things. Had she turned inward to admire her own detachment, she would have disturbed the very stillness in which she stood. The soul that continually observes its own spiritual excellence remains occupied with itself, however noble its intentions may appear.

I would lead you into a poverty deeper than the surrender of possessions, achievements, or desires. Even the wish to become a worthy dwelling for God must finally be released. For if you imagine that you have prepared a place in which God may accomplish his work, you still claim something as your own. In the deepest poverty, God himself is the dwelling, the worker, and the work. Nothing remains for the soul to possess or defend.

Therefore, cease measuring how detached you have become. Practice goodness, embrace humility, and surrender what binds you, but do not turn these virtues into treasures to admire. Let God accomplish his work without demanding that you witness your own perfection. For the soul that has relinquished even its claim to spiritual freedom is ready to receive the fullness of divine life.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *I release even my striving*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let Your work be wholly Yours*
