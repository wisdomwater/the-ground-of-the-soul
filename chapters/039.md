## Day 39 - Hindrances on the Way

> *Even the holiest things may become obstacles when the soul clings to them instead of passing through them to God.*

When our Lord told his disciples that it was necessary for him to depart, they could scarcely understand how his absence might benefit them. Yet he declared that unless he went away, the Holy Spirit would not come to them. I tell you that this departure reveals a mystery concerning the soul. Even the visible presence of Christ, precious beyond every earthly gift, could become a hindrance if his disciples clung to what their senses perceived and failed to receive the fullness of his divine life.

There are many things that hinder the soul on its journey toward God. Some are plainly disordered desires, through which you seek satisfaction in passing pleasures. Others appear holy and worthy of devotion. You may receive the sacraments, practice repentance, and cherish the humanity of Christ, yet become so attached to their outward forms that you neglect the inward truth they reveal. The sign is good, but its purpose is to lead you into the reality it signifies.

Consider also the soul that continually examines its past offenses, returning again and again to sins already confessed. Such a person may mistake endless self-examination for humility, while remaining occupied with the very self from which grace offers freedom. True repentance should turn you toward God and make you ready for renewed obedience, rather than imprisoning you in what has passed.

Therefore, honor the sacred things through which God draws you, but do not make them the boundaries of your spiritual life. Let every prayer, sacrament, and act of repentance lead you more deeply into Christ, in whom humanity and divinity are united. For the soul advances when it receives the gift without clinging to its outward form, allowing the Holy Spirit to bring it toward the fullness of divine truth.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Lead me beyond what I cling to*
>
> **Exhale:** *Into the fullness of Your life*
