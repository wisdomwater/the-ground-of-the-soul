## Day 25 - The Light That Is Not Ours

> *The soul may seek the light of God, but only God can give the light by which he is truly known.*

I tell you that there are three lights by which the soul may come to know God. The first is the light of nature, through which your understanding recognizes that created things do not possess their existence from themselves. You see goodness scattered throughout creation and reason that there must be a source in which all goodness is gathered. This natural light is a noble gift, for it awakens the soul to the One from whom all things proceed.

The second light is the light of faith. Through faith, the soul receives truths that its natural understanding could never discover by itself. You learn that the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are three Persons sharing one divine nature. No image drawn from creation can fully reveal this mystery, for the life of God exceeds every likeness your intellect can form. Faith therefore opens your understanding to what reason alone cannot reach.

But I speak also of a third light, the light of divine glory. In this light, the soul receives a knowledge of God without anything standing between them. Such knowledge cannot be produced by study, reasoning, or even the strength of faith considered merely as your own activity. God himself must illuminate the soul, for only the divine understanding can comprehend the divine nature in its fullness.

Therefore, honor the light of reason and cherish the gift of faith, but remain humble before the greater light that comes from God alone. Do not mistake the limits of your understanding for the limits of divine reality. Allow your soul to become receptive to what exceeds its powers, trusting that the One who awakens your desire to know him is also the source of the light in which he may be known.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Illuminate my soul*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let me see by Your light*
