## Day 5 - The Castle of the Soul

> *The soul becomes a dwelling for God when it is free to receive him and fruitful enough to reveal him.*

I tell you that the soul is like a castle into which our Lord Jesus desires to enter. The Gospel speaks of a woman who received him into her home, and I see in this encounter a mystery concerning the soul. For the soul that welcomes God must become like a virgin, free from every attachment that would claim its heart. This purity is not a matter of having never encountered the world, but of standing before God without possessiveness, ready to receive whatever he may give.

You may possess great knowledge, remember many experiences, and carry countless images within your understanding. None of these need prevent the divine presence, provided you do not cling to them as possessions. Even the most excellent spiritual disciplines can become hindrances when you grow so attached to them that you cannot respond freely to God. The soul must remain available to the divine will as it reveals itself in each present moment.

Yet I say something further: it is good for the soul to receive God, but better still that God should become fruitful within it. For this reason I speak of the soul not only as a virgin but also as a wife. What is received in freedom must be brought forth in gratitude, love, and the living expression of divine goodness. A soul that receives many gifts but bears no fruit has not yet entered the fullness of its calling.

Therefore, let your inward castle remain open to God, neither crowded by your attachments nor closed within itself. Receive the divine presence with a free heart, and allow what is born within you to become visible through the life you live. For the deepest union with God is never barren. It is a life continually receiving and continually bearing fruit.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Make my soul Your dwelling*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let Your love bear fruit in me*
