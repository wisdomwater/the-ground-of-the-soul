## Day 30 - The One Beyond Multiplicity

> *The soul draws near to divine unity when it releases the divisions by which it has learned to understand the world.*

I tell you that God is one, yet this oneness is unlike anything you encounter among created things. You may gather many stones into a single pile or unite many voices in a single song, but such unity remains composed of parts. God's oneness admits no such division, for nothing can be added to him, and nothing can be taken away. He possesses all things within the simplicity of his divine being, without movement, change, or need of anything beyond himself.

Consider how your understanding divides the world into countless distinctions. You recognize one thing by comparing it with another, and you come to know yourself through the qualities that distinguish you from those around you. Such knowledge serves you well among creatures, but it cannot carry you into the divine ground. For God is not one being standing alongside others, but the source in which every creature receives its existence. What appears divided in creation has its eternal beginning in the unity of God.

I call this divine oneness the denial of denials, for even the distinctions by which you seek to describe God must finally be surrendered. You may call him good or true, yet his being exceeds the qualities these names express. The soul that would receive God must therefore become free from all that it adds through thought and desire, allowing its deepest ground to receive what no created image can contain.

Therefore, hear the invitation spoken to the soul: Friend, come up higher. Rise beyond the narrow boundaries of possessiveness and the divisions that occupy your understanding. Become inwardly simple, steadfast, and free, so that your soul may rest in the One from whom all things proceed and in whom they find their perfection.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Gather me into Your oneness*
>
> **Exhale:** *Free me from all division*
