## Day 26 - The Speculative Intellect

> *The soul sees most clearly when its scattered powers are gathered into the simplicity of God.*

I tell you that the soul must possess three things if it is to attain a higher and truer vision of God. First, it must possess its own mind. Second, that mind must be free. Third, it must become capable of contemplation. These are not separate accomplishments, but movements toward a single inward freedom in which the soul becomes receptive to divine truth.

To possess your mind is to gather your scattered powers into unity. Within you is a spiritual depth where things are known without their material forms, and a light through which the understanding perceives them. Yet your attention is often divided among the many things that promise satisfaction. When you cease to demand fulfillment from created things, your inward powers begin to find their proper harmony. You become less governed by what happens around you and more firmly established in the ground of your being.

The second requirement is freedom. By this I mean a soul that is no longer bound by possessiveness or confined by its own desires. Such freedom prepares the intellect for its highest work, which is to contemplate God. Here the soul discovers a goodness exceeding every particular good, a delight surpassing every pleasure, and a unity in which all its scattered longings find their source.

Consider how a circle remains ordered around its center. So too the soul must learn to move without departing from the divine center toward which it is drawn. When God's goodness touches your highest power, you are drawn beyond the narrow boundaries of self-concern into the truth from which all things proceed.

Therefore, gather your attention, release what binds you, and allow your understanding to become clear. For the highest vision belongs to the soul whose many powers have become united in God.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Gather my scattered mind*
>
> **Exhale:** *Center my soul in You*
