## Day 42 - The Soul and Its Rank

> *The soul's greatness lies not in what it possesses, but in its capacity to receive the fullness of God.*

I tell you that when God created the soul, he turned toward himself and fashioned her in his own likeness. Among all created things, nothing bears this likeness in quite the same manner. The soul cannot be measured by the dimensions of the body, nor can her nature be captured in any outward form. Though she dwells within time, her highest powers reach toward what is eternal. Such is the dignity of the soul that she may receive the very life of God.

Consider how the soul gives life to the body. Through her presence, the eyes see, the ears hear, and the limbs move, even while the mind may be occupied elsewhere. Yet the soul possesses powers more noble than those by which she governs the senses. Through her higher understanding, she may turn toward divine things, receiving a light that no earthly creature can bestow. When she neglects these higher powers and becomes absorbed entirely in passing pleasures, she lives beneath the dignity for which she was created.

But do not mistake this nobility for independence from God. God possesses his being from himself, while the soul receives everything she is from him. Her greatness lies in this wondrous capacity for receiving, for nothing less than God himself can satisfy the depth of her longing. When divine grace enters the soul, it awakens understanding, strengthens love, and draws the will toward freedom.

Therefore, learn to recognize the dignity of the life within you. Do not measure the soul by the weakness of the body or the changing circumstances of the world. Turn your highest powers toward God, allowing his light to illuminate your understanding and his love to guide your will. For the soul fulfills her noble calling when she becomes wholly receptive to the One whose likeness she bears.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Fashioned in Your likeness*

> **Exhale:** *Fill my soul with Your life*
