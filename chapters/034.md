## Day 34 - Letting Go of What We Claim

> *The soul becomes truly poor when it no longer claims even its own goodness as a possession.*

I tell you that spiritual poverty is not measured by how little you possess, but by how completely you have surrendered the claim that anything belongs to you. You may give away your wealth, abandon your comforts, and devote yourself to holy works, yet still remain rich in your own estimation. For the soul may cling to its virtue as tightly as another clings to gold, finding satisfaction in what it has accomplished and imagining that these achievements make it worthy of God.

Consider the soul that has begun to recognize the goodness of Christ. As it beholds his life, it becomes aware of the distance between divine perfection and its own imperfect efforts. Even its most admirable works appear small before such love. This recognition need not lead to despair, for it may open the soul to a deeper humility in which it ceases to depend upon its own merit. What once seemed a possession becomes an offering freely surrendered.

Yet there is a greater poverty still. The soul must become free even from the desire to understand or measure its union with God. It discovers that divine reality exceeds everything it can know or accomplish. In this unknowing, the soul learns to receive all things without attachment, using what is needed and remaining equally free when those things are absent. Its peace no longer rests upon what it has gained, preserved, or achieved.

Therefore, release your claim upon the goodness you have cultivated and the progress you believe you have made. Let your works arise from divine love rather than the desire to establish your worth. For the soul that has nothing to defend may become wholly receptive to God, whose fullness is received most deeply where possessiveness has come to an end.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *All goodness comes from You*
>
> **Exhale:** *I release what I claim as mine*
