## Day 29 - The Hidden Source

> *All things proceed from God, are sustained through God, and find their fulfillment in returning to God.*

Saint Paul declares that all things are from God, through God, and in God. I would have you consider these words carefully, for they reveal a mystery concerning the divine life and the origin of all creation. From the Father, all things have their beginning. Through the Son, all things receive their being. And in the Holy Spirit, all things are gathered toward the fulfillment for which they were made. These are not three separate works, but the one divine activity of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Consider the sun shining upon the earth. Its rays illuminate countless creatures, yet the sun loses nothing of its brightness. So too God communicates life to everything that exists without diminishing the fullness of his own being. Every creature receives its existence from this inexhaustible source, and every creature moves according to its nature toward the perfection for which it was created. What comes forth from God is sustained by God and finds its deepest meaning in God.

Yet I tell you that there is a difference between seeing creatures merely as creatures and recognizing them in their divine source. Your outward senses perceive the beauty of a flower, the nourishment of bread, and the pleasures of the world. Your inward understanding may recognize these as gifts of God. But in the deepest ground of the soul, you may come to behold them in the eternal life from which they proceed.

Therefore, learn to look upon creation without becoming imprisoned by its outward forms. Receive each thing with gratitude, recognizing the divine goodness that gives it existence. For the world is not separated from its source, and your own soul is called to return in understanding and love to the One from whom all life continually flows.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *From You all life proceeds*

> **Exhale:** *To You my soul returns*
