## Day 16 - The Virgin Soul

> *The soul becomes fruitful in God when it receives the divine Word with the openness of a virgin heart.*

When the angel Gabriel came to Mary, he greeted her as one full of grace, declaring that the Lord was with her. I tell you that these words were not spoken to Mary alone, but to every soul that longs to receive God. For Mary first conceived the divine Word spiritually before she bore him in the flesh. What took place within her is therefore a mystery in which your own soul is invited to participate.

You may wonder what it means for the soul to become a virgin. I do not speak merely of bodily purity, but of an inward freedom through which the soul becomes wholly available to God. A virgin soul is one that does not cling possessively to its thoughts, desires, or achievements. It stands before God without demanding that the divine presence conform to its expectations. In this openness, the soul becomes capable of receiving what no human effort can produce.

Consider the wonder of the Incarnation. God did not remain distant from the human condition, but took our nature upon himself, joining what is divine to what is human. I would have you understand the greatness of this love, for the eternal Word desires to be born not only in the world, but also within you. The same divine generosity that filled Mary with grace reaches toward every soul prepared to receive it.

Therefore, make room within yourself for this sacred birth. Let your heart become free from whatever would claim its deepest allegiance, and receive the Word with the humility and openness of Mary. For the soul that welcomes God does not merely hold a beautiful thought or cherish a holy memory. It becomes a living dwelling in which divine life may take root and bear fruit.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Make my soul open to You*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let Your Word take root in me*
