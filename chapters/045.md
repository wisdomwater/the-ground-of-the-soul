## Day 45 - Young Man, Arise

> *When the eternal Word calls the soul to rise, what was lifeless within it awakens to the light of God.*

I tell you of the young man whom our Lord encountered as he was being carried from the city of Nain. He was the only son of a widow, and those who loved him had surrendered him to death. But Christ approached, touched the bier, and commanded, “Young man, I say unto thee, arise.” At once the young man sat up and began to speak. Consider this wonder, for I see in it a mystery concerning the awakening of your own soul.

The widow represents the understanding, and her son signifies the highest intellect, that noble power through which the soul may receive divine light. When the soul becomes lost in imperfection, this inward power lies as though dead, unable to perceive the truth for which it was created. Yet when the eternal Word addresses the soul, divine life awakens within it. What had grown silent begins to speak, answering the Word through which it has received new life.

You may ask what such an awakening requires. I tell you that understanding and love must both serve the soul on its journey. Understanding seeks the truth, while love draws the soul toward the goodness it discovers. Yet at the highest point, even these noble powers must yield to the work of God. The soul can no longer raise itself by its own strength, for what it formerly sought to accomplish, God now accomplishes within it.

Therefore, hear the command of Christ as spoken also to you. Do not remain confined within the familiar limits of your understanding or the habits that have governed your life. Allow the eternal Word to awaken what lies dormant within your soul, drawing you into the divine life where all your powers find their fulfillment.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Speak Your Word within me*
>
> **Exhale:** *Awaken me to Your life*
