## Day 23 - The Image and Its Object

> *The soul bears an image of God, yet the image finds its fulfillment only in the One it reflects.*

Consider how your eyes perceive the world. When you look upon a stone or a tree, you do not receive the thing itself into your sight, but an image through which it becomes known to you. So too your understanding approaches the divine through likenesses, gathering what it can from the beauty and goodness of created things. Yet I tell you that the soul is made for a knowledge more immediate than any image can provide.

Think of a mirror placed before your face. The likeness that appears within it depends entirely upon the one standing before it. The reflection possesses no independent life, for its existence is bound to what it reveals. I tell you that the image and its object belong together in a mysterious unity. So it is with the soul's deepest likeness to God, which cannot be understood apart from the divine source that gives it life.

Yet there is a greater wonder. The eternal Word is himself the perfect image of the Father, and through this Word the soul may know God without any created image standing between them. Here the intellect reaches beyond the forms it ordinarily uses to understand the world. It becomes free from the limitations of time and place, seeking the divine truth in its purity and simplicity.

Therefore, do not become satisfied with the reflections through which you have learned to recognize God. Receive their beauty with gratitude, but allow your understanding to reach toward the One they reveal. For the soul was fashioned to receive more than distant likenesses. Through the eternal Word, it may encounter the living reality from which every true image draws its light.
