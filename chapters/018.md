## Day 18 - The Word God Speaks

> *In speaking his eternal Word, the Father gives not merely knowledge of himself, but the fullness of his own life.*

I tell you that the Father speaks only one Word, and in this Word he knows himself and all that he has brought forth. Do not imagine that God speaks as you do, forming one thought after another or choosing words to express what was previously hidden. The Father speaks his Word eternally, bringing forth the Son from the fullness of his own nature. This speaking belongs so completely to the divine life that the Father cannot cease to speak, any more than he can cease to be God.

Consider the difference between an earthly father and his child. The father gives something of his nature to the child, yet each possesses a separate life. One may die while the other continues to live. But the heavenly Father and his eternal Son possess one undivided life and being. What the Father communicates to the Son is not a portion of himself, but the fullness of his divine nature.

And now I tell you something wonderful: when the Father reveals this eternal Word within your soul, he gives you a share in that same divine life. He does not merely offer knowledge about himself or a distant promise of blessedness. In the light of the Word, the soul is drawn into the unity in which the Father knows himself and all things. There, distinctions that appear so fixed in ordinary understanding no longer divide what God gathers into one.

Therefore, do not seek the divine Word as though it were a message sent from far away. Become receptive to the light in which God eternally knows and gives himself. For the Word spoken by the Father is also the life he desires to communicate within the deepest ground of your soul.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Speak Your eternal Word*
>
> **Exhale:** *Let me share in Your life*
