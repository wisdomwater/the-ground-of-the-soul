## Day 35 - Nothing to Own, Nothing to Prove

> *The soul that has nothing to prove is free to receive everything as a gift.*

I tell you that a person may perform many holy works and yet remain far from true freedom. You may fast, pray, give generously, and devote yourself to the service of others, while secretly measuring your worth by what you have accomplished. Such works may be good, but they do not by themselves reveal the condition of your soul. The surest sign of spiritual growth is that eternal things become increasingly precious to you, while the passing things of this world lose their power to command your heart.

Consider a person who possesses great wealth and gives it all away for love of God. This is a noble deed, yet I say there is something greater still: to hold such wealth so lightly that its presence or absence makes no difference to the soul's inward freedom. For one may surrender everything outwardly while continuing to possess it inwardly through memory, longing, or pride. True poverty begins when the heart no longer depends upon what it has or what it has surrendered.

This freedom reaches even into your spiritual life. You may become attached to your goodness, your understanding, or the progress you believe you have made. You may hesitate to act because you fear making a mistake, examining every possibility until the present opportunity has passed. But I would have you direct your will toward God and undertake the work before you without becoming imprisoned by your own self-examination.

Therefore, let your good works flow from a heart that seeks neither recognition nor possession. Receive what comes, release what passes, and perform the next faithful act without demanding certainty about its reward. For the soul that belongs wholly to God has no need to establish its worth through anything it can claim as its own.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *I have nothing to prove*
>
> **Exhale:** *All I am rests in You*
