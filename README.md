# The Ground of the Soul: 100 Days with Meister Eckhart

This project is a 100-day contemplative journey through the mystical teachings of Meister Eckhart, the 14th-century Dominican theologian and philosopher. Drawing from his sermons and spiritual writings, it explores the divine presence within the soul, the eternal birth of God, detachment, spiritual freedom, and union with the Divine. Each daily reading offers a modern, accessible reflection accompanied by an original aphorism and breath prayer, inviting readers to move beyond intellectual understanding into a deeper awareness of the sacred presence at the ground of their being.
